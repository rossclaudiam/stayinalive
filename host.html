🎭 The Setup

It's Halloween, 1977. The Mirror Ball — Manhattan's hottest disco — is throwing its one-year anniversary bash. Everyone who's anyone is on the dance floor: fading starlets, a mob fixer, an astrology columnist, a trust-fund playboy, a funk preacher with a side hustle. The club's charming owner, "Disco" Dino Fontana, has invited his inner circle up to the VIP lounge for a champagne toast at 8:30.

At 8:31, Dino crumples to the floor. Somebody has poisoned his signature pink champagne. Luckily, Lieutenant Harriet "Harry" Vance of the NYPD is in the room — off-duty, in a jumpsuit, but still a cop.

Dino lingers long enough to witness his own crime scene from the other side. The guests have until 10 PM to figure out who did it — before Harry does.

⏰ Timeline of the Evening
  • 7:30 PMDoors open. Guests arrive in costume. Point them at the guest landing page to type their character name. Disco music, mocktails/cocktails, small bites. You stay out-of-character.
  • 8:15 PMIn-character mingling begins. Announce: "The Mirror Ball's anniversary toast is in 15 minutes — everybody find your groove."
  • 8:30 PM🚨 THE MURDER. Dino staggers out, clutches his chest, delivers one line, drops. You step forward as Lt. Vance. Advance to Round 1.
  • 8:45 PMRound 1 clue. Mel reveals Dino was changing his will tomorrow. Guests tap "Round 1" on their phone to unlock a new clue.
  • 9:00 PM👻 Ghost emerges. Dino returns in a sheet or white boa. Guests can interview him. Advance to Round 2.
  • 9:15 PMRound 2 clue. Bartender confirms only one other person touched the pink bottle — someone who went "to grab a jacket."
  • 9:40 PMRound 3 clue. Letter from "Pearl" found in Dino's pocket, mentions "our Victoria."
  • 9:50 PMAccusations. Each guest writes their guess on a slip. Prize for correct answers.
  • 10:00 PM🎉 The reveal. You name the killer. Dino rises from the dead. Dessert, dancing, prizes.

🎤 Your Lines (Lt. Harry Vance)

Costume: Flared pantsuit, aviators, badge on a lanyard. Think Columbo meets Pam Grier — dry, warm, a little bemused.

At 8:30 — the body drops
"Alright. Nobody panic, nobody leave, and nobody — nobody — touches that champagne glass. I'm Lieutenant Vance, NYPD. I was off-duty, now I'm on. Mel, you were his lawyer — you first."
At 8:45 — Round 1
"Mel just told me something interesting. Our victim was changing his will tomorrow. Which means somebody in this room was about to get a very ugly phone call. Think about who. I'll be around."
At 9:15 — Round 2
"I just talked to the bartender. Dino's pink bottle lives behind the bar — his bottle, nobody else's. One person besides Dino touched it tonight. Someone who left this room to 'grab a jacket.' Who leaves a party like this to get a jacket in October? Chew on that."
At 9:40 — Round 3
"Found this in his pocket. 1972. A woman named Pearl writes about 'our Victoria' and asks Dino to acknowledge her. Pearl died in '72. The kid would be — what, nineteen now? Twenty? Keep an eye out for a Victoria."
At 10:00 — the reveal
"Vicki Velvet is Victoria. She's Dino's daughter. She was promised the club. He was about to pull it out from under her. She works the coat check — ten feet from the bar — and she slipped behind the bar when she went to 'grab a jacket.' She had the motive, she had the access, and she had Dino's heart medication from a bottle she lifted out of Dr. Feelgood's bag last week. Case closed. Someone put on 'I Will Survive' — the dead man wants to dance."

👻 Ghost Husband — Rules of Engagement

At 9:00 PM, Dino "comes back" as a ghost. Give him a white blazer, a sheet, sunglasses + white boa — something clearly post-mortem.

What he knows (and will say if asked)

  • He was drinking his signature pink champagne all evening — his special bottle, kept behind the bar.
  • He felt the first wave of dizziness around 8:20, after the second glass.
  • He saw Vicki by the bar earlier, but she was always by the bar.
  • He had a letter in his pocket from Pearl, his old girlfriend. He never told anyone.
  • He was going to change his will tomorrow to cut Vicki out and sell to Tito.
  • He is not surprised he's dead. A little surprised it was poison and not a baseball bat.

What he does NOT know

  • Who actually poisoned the glass.
  • That Vicki is his daughter. (Reveal at 10 PM — react accordingly.)

How to answer

  • Truthful about every fact above. Playful and evasive about embarrassing stuff (money you owe, stolen heart meds, people you've publicly screwed over).
  • If asked about Pearl: "Pearl? Oh, Pearl was… before your time, honey. Beautiful girl. Broke my heart. I was young and stupid."
  • Ghost can drink and dance. Ghost does not have to be morbid.

🚨 Solution (don't let guests see this)

Murderer: Vicki Velvet.

Motive: Dino was her father. He abandoned her mother Pearl (died 1972, broke). He promised Vicki the club. Last week he told her he was selling it to Tito and cutting her out. The new will gets signed tomorrow — tonight was her only window.

Means: Powdered sedative + crushed heart medication (she stole a bottle from Dr. Feelgood's bag at a party last week) — dropped into Dino's private pink champagne bottle behind the bar.

Opportunity: She works coat check — just off the bar — and "went to grab a jacket" between songs.

Red herrings: Tito (Dino's death ruins his deal); Lola (publicly cut from the documentary, bitter but not a killer); Chip (debts, but Dino's death helps him since the debt dies too); Starla (motive but on-stage during tampering); Reverend (being blackmailed); Bunny (Dino was about to invest in her agency — his death is her financial catastrophe); Mona (lucky guess).
